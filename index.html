About TZ Live FM

How it works: Browsers cannot receive analog FM radio waves. This app plays the live internet streams of real FM and online radio stations. Frequencies are only shown when they are part of a station's real name.

Your privacy: Your location is used only on your device to query nearby stations and is never stored on any server by this app. A rounded copy is kept locally for 24 hours so the app starts faster.

Sound: Audio plays through your device's normal speaker — nothing else is needed.

About me: I'm T Zerone (Tarhit), the creator of TZ Live FM. I built this app to make listening to live radio from around the world simple, fast and free.
